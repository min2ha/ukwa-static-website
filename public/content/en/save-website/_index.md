# Save a UK Website

Help preserve the UK's digital heritage by nominating websites for archiving. Your nominations ensure that important UK web content is captured and preserved for future generations.

## How to Nominate a Website

You can nominate any UK-based website or web content that you believe should be preserved. This includes:

- Government and public sector websites, such as official government departments, local councils, and public services
- Cultural institutions including museums, libraries, galleries, and heritage sites
- Educational resources from universities, schools, and educational organisations
- Community websites run by local groups, charities, and community organisations
- Business websites belonging to companies, industry associations, and professional bodies
- Personal blogs and websites created by individual writers, artists, and archivists

## What Makes a Good Nomination?

Consider nominating websites that are:

- At risk of being lost, taken down, or substantially changed
- Of historical or cultural significance to the UK
- Important for research, journalism, or education
- Representative of UK digital culture or society
- Documenting significant events, movements, or public debates
- Creating unique or valuable content not held elsewhere

## Nomination Process

1. Identify the website

Note the full URL and any specific pages or sections of particular importance. If the site is time-sensitive or at risk, please mention this.

2. Provide context

Explain why this website should be archived and what makes it significant. The more detail you can give, the better we can assess its priority.

3. Submit your nomination

Contact us directly at web-archivist@bl.uk with the URL and your reasons for nomination. We welcome nominations from members of the public, researchers, and partner organisations.

4. Confirmation

We will review your nomination and, if appropriate, add it to our archiving queue. You may receive a response confirming whether the site has been scheduled for capture.

## Archiving Schedule

We archive websites at different frequencies depending on their nature and importance:

- High priority sites, such as those covering live events or rapidly changing content, are captured daily or weekly
- Regularly updated sites, such as news outlets and active blogs, are captured monthly
- Stable sites with infrequently changing content are captured quarterly or annually
- Event-based captures are carried out during significant events, elections, or public announcements

## Permission and Copyright

We work within the UK's legal deposit regulations and intellectual property laws. Website owners are welcome to contact us directly to:

- Request archiving of their own site
- Control the level of public access to archived versions
- Update their archiving preferences or opt out of public display

## Contact and Further Information

If you have questions about nominating a website or would like to learn more about our archiving process, please contact us at web-archivist@bl.uk or visit the Information section of this website for further details about how the UK Web Archive operates.
