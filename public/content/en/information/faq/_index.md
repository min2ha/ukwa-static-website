# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is the UK Web Archive?

The UK Web Archive (UKWA) collects and preserves websites published in the UK. We aim to ensure that the UK's web history is available to researchers and the public both now and in the future.

## Why do you archive websites?

Websites are an important record of contemporary life, culture, and knowledge. Unlike books or journals, websites can disappear without notice. By archiving them, we preserve this digital heritage for future generations.

## How can I access archived websites?

You can search our archive using the URL search box on the home page. Access to some archived content is restricted to Legal Deposit Library reading rooms under copyright law.

## Who can submit a website for archiving?

Anyone can nominate a website for inclusion in the archive using our Save a UK Website form. Our curators review nominations and may add them to our collection.

## How often are websites archived?

Most UK websites are crawled at least once a year during our annual domain crawl. High-priority sites, such as news sources, may be collected daily or more frequently.

## Can I opt out of being archived?

Website owners may request removal of their content via our Notice and Takedown procedure. Please see our [Notice and Takedown](/information/notice-and-takedown) page for details.
