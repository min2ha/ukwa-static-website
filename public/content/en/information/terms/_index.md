# Terms and Conditions

## General

The UK Web Archive (UKWA) is operated by the British Library Board, a body corporate established under the British Library Act of 27 July 1972, of 96 Euston Road, London NW1 2DB, United Kingdom, on behalf of all six UK legal deposit libraries:

- [Bodleian Libraries, Oxford](https://www.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/)
- [British Library](https://www.bl.uk/)
- [Cambridge University Libraries](https://www.lib.cam.ac.uk/)
- [National Library of Scotland](https://www.nls.uk/)
- [National Library of Wales](https://www.library.wales/)
- [Trinity College, Dublin](https://www.tcd.ie/)

## Information about Access and Use

The UK Web Archive operates under the Legal Deposit Libraries (Non-Print Works) Regulations 2013. These regulations allow the six UK Legal Deposit Libraries to collect and preserve UK-published websites for long-term preservation and research.

## Legal deposit, copyright, and access

Most archived websites in the UK Web Archive are protected by copyright and are collected without altering the rights of the original content creators. Where material is made available for public viewing online, this is done in accordance with legal deposit legislation and relevant licensing conditions.

Access to archived websites is subject to legal restrictions. Much of the archived collection can only be viewed on-site within the reading rooms of the UK Legal Deposit Libraries. Where additional permission has been granted by the rights holder, some content may be made available online through this website.

Certain materials may also be available under open licences, such as the Open Government Licence or Crown Copyright terms, where applicable. In all cases, users must respect applicable copyright and licensing conditions.

## Archival nature of the content

The UK Web Archive preserves websites as historical records of the internet at a specific point in time. Archived websites are not updated and do not reflect current information. Content should therefore be understood in its original historical context.

The inclusion of a website in the archive does not imply endorsement, approval, or recommendation of its content by the UK Web Archive or any of the Legal Deposit Libraries. Responsibility for the content of archived websites remains with the original publishers.

## Use of archived material

The UK Web Archive is intended for research, education, and cultural preservation. Users are responsible for how they interpret and use archived content, particularly when it may be out of date or no longer reflect current views, services, or information.

## Contact

If you have questions about access, rights, or reuse of archived material, please contact us at web-archivist@bl.uk.
