# Notice and Takedown

The UK Web Archive collects and preserves publicly available UK websites under the Legal Deposit Libraries Act 2003. If you believe that content archived by us infringes your rights or causes harm, you may request its removal.

## Grounds for takedown requests

We will consider requests on the following grounds:

- **Copyright infringement** — you own the copyright in the material and did not consent to it being archived
- **Personal data** — the content contains your personal data and you have a right to its removal
- **Defamation** — the content is demonstrably false and defamatory
- **Other legal grounds** — content that is unlawful under UK law

## How to submit a request

To submit a notice and takedown request, please contact us with the following information:

1. Your name and contact details
2. The URL(s) of the archived content you wish to have removed
3. The grounds for your request
4. A statement that you are the rights holder or are authorised to act on their behalf

**Email:** web-archivist@bl.uk  
**Subject line:** Notice and Takedown Request

## What happens next

We aim to acknowledge your request within **5 working days**. We will review the request and, where appropriate, remove or restrict access to the content while the matter is investigated.

We may decline requests that do not meet the criteria above, or where we have a legal obligation to preserve the content.

## Appeals

If you are unhappy with our decision, you may escalate your complaint to the British Library's legal team by writing to: The British Library, 96 Euston Road, London, NW1 2DB.
