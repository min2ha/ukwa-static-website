# Accessibility Statement for the UK Web Archive

The British Library is committed to making its websites accessible, in accordance with the Public Sector Bodies (Websites and Mobile Applications) (No. 2) Accessibility Regulations 2018.

## Compliance status

This website is **partially compliant** with the [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/) AA standard. The non-compliances and exemptions are listed below.

## Non-accessible content

We are aware of the following accessibility issues and are working to address them:

- Some older archived web pages may not meet accessibility standards; these are preserved as historical records.
- Some images may lack adequate alternative text.
- PDF documents in the archive may not be fully accessible.

## What we're doing to improve

We regularly review our website for accessibility and aim to fix issues as they are identified. We work to ensure all new content meets WCAG 2.1 AA standards.

## Feedback and contact

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on this site, please contact us:

- **Email:** web-archivist@bl.uk
- **Response time:** We aim to respond within 5 working days.

## Enforcement procedure

If you are not happy with our response, contact the [Equality Advisory and Support Service (EASS)](https://www.equalityadvisoryservice.com/).

This statement was prepared on 29 April 2026.
