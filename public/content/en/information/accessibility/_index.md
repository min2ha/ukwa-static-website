# Accessibility statement for the UK Web Archive

This accessibility statement applies to the UK Web Archive website: [UK Web Archive website](https://www.webarchive.org.uk)

## Compliance status

This website has been tested against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) version 2.2 level AA standard.

A full accessibility review of the website was carried out by \[Resilien in August 2026\]. In addition, a representative sample of the website was validated internally by the British Library using automated testing tools including WAVE and Google Lighthouse.

For full details of the British Library’s broader accessibility approach, see the [British Library accessibility statement](https://www.bl.uk/accessibility-statement). [\[bl.uk\]](https://www.bl.uk/accessibility-statement)

## Scope of this statement

This accessibility statement applies only to the UK Web Archive website at  
[UK Web Archive](https://www.webarchive.org.uk).

It does **not** apply to the content of archived websites available through the UK Web Archive.

## Accessibility of archived websites

The UK Web Archive provides access to archived copies of websites. The content of these websites is created by third parties and is captured at specific points in time.

As a result:

- the UK Web Archive, the British Library, and the Legal Deposit Libraries do **not control the accessibility** of archived content

- archived websites may not meet current accessibility standards, including WCAG 2.2 AA

- users may encounter a wide range of accessibility levels when accessing archived material

We are unable to modify archived content to improve accessibility, as it is preserved in its original form for research and record-keeping purposes.

## Feedback and contact information

We are committed to improving the accessibility of the UK Web Archive website.

If you experience any accessibility issues with the website itself (not the archived content), or if you need information in a different format, please contact us:

- Email: web-archivist@bl.uk

## Preparation of this accessibility statement

This statement was prepared following accessibility testing carried out in August 2026.  
The website was tested through:

- an independent audit carried out by Resilient

- internal validation of a representative sample of pages using WAVE and Google Lighthouse

The statement will be reviewed regularly and updated as necessary.
