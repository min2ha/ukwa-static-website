# Cookie Policy

## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences and understand how you use it.

## How we use cookies

The UK Web Archive website uses cookies to:

- Remember your language preference
- Remember your theme preference (light or dark mode)
- Understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it (analytics)

We do not use cookies to track you across other websites or to serve advertising.

## Essential cookies

Some cookies are essential for the website to function. These cannot be disabled and do not store any personally identifiable information.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|---------|----------|
| `theme` | Stores your preferred colour scheme | 1 year |
| `lang`  | Stores your preferred language | 1 year |

## Analytics cookies

With your consent, we use anonymised analytics to understand site usage. No personal data is collected or shared with third parties.

## Managing cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. For more information, visit [aboutcookies.org](https://www.aboutcookies.org/).
