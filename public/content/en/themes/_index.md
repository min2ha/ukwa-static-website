# Themes

The UK Web Archive builds focused collections around particular events, topics, and themes — curated by our subject specialists and often developed in collaboration with partner organisations.

Thematic collections allow us to capture a wider picture of how specific topics evolved on the UK web: from public health campaigns and sporting events to political milestones and cultural moments.

## Browse our themes

- **Politics and Government** — Elections, referendums, policy announcements, and parliamentary records.
- **Health and Medicine** — Public health campaigns, NHS milestones, and pandemic response.
- **Arts and Culture** — Literature, music, film, and creative heritage.
- **Science and Technology** — Innovation, research institutions, and digital society.
- **Environment and Climate** — Environmental campaigns, climate policy, and sustainability.
- **Education** — Schools, universities, and learning resources across the UK.
- **Sport** — Major sporting events, clubs, and national competitions.
- **News and Media** — UK news organisations and their evolving digital presence.

## Nominate a website for a theme

If you know of a website that belongs in one of our themed collections, you can suggest it using our [Save a UK Website](/save-website) form. Our curators review all nominations.

## For researchers

Researchers can request enhanced access to thematic datasets. Please contact us at web-archivist@bl.uk for more information.
